## Brand & Style

This design system blends cheerful, high-spirited childhood wonder with reliable, industrial-grade commerce credibility. It serves two interconnected audiences: retail parents looking for safe, joyful, well-made toys, and B2B distributors or shop owners seeking competitive wholesale volume, predictable supply chains, and verified manufacturing standards.

The visual style is **Tactile Modern Commerce** infused with friendly consumer cues:
- **Warmth & Safety First**: Soft, rounded surfaces, smooth pill corners, and pastel tinting replace harsh borders to evoke child-safe tactility.
- **Dual-Channel Distinction**: Retail consumer interactions (Add to Cart, Buy Now, Reviews) lean into high-energy coral magenta, while wholesale business touchpoints (Get a Sample, B2B Portal, Wholesale Pricing Tiers) utilize structured electric blue and emerald verification accents.
- **Clean Structure**: Information density remains high and scannable for B2B procurement without sacrificing the celebratory, emotional charm essential for toy merchandising.

## Colors

The palette balances energetic retail action, wholesale authority, safety signaling, and comforting pastel bases:

- **Primary (`#E11D48`)**: High-saturation rose-coral used for primary retail CTAs ("Add to Cart", "Shop Now"), discount chips, promotional banners, and consumer highlights.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`)**: Confident electric royal blue driving wholesale actions ("Get a Sample", "B2B Wholesale Portal", primary step actions, and link states).
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`)**: Warm marigold amber for star ratings, shipping incentives, and limited-edition badges.
- **Verification Emerald (`#10B981`)**: Dedicated to child safety endorsements, BIS/non-toxic certification badges, and wholesale in-stock stock availability.
- **Backgrounds & Tinted Canvases**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for crisp product cards and modals, cushioned by soft sky pastels (`#EFF6FF`), delicate blush tint (`#FFF1F2`), cream sand (`#FFFBEB`), and structural canvas slate (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Text & Borders**: Primary text anchors on deep slate (`#0F172A`), muted supporting labels rest at `#64748B`, with hairline container outlines at `#E2E8F0`.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels to retain geometric balance with organic, friendly terminal cuts.

- **Hero & Section Headers**: Rendered in Bold and Extra-Bold weights (700/800) with slight negative tracking to give toy category names and campaign slogans an energetic, punchy poster feel.
- **Numbers, Pricing, & Spec Keys**: Prices feature bold numerals paired with stacked fractional/currency signs and clear discount strikes (`line-through`) in muted slate.
- **Micro-copy & Badges**: Set in uppercase or capitalized Title case with positive letter tracking (0.02em to 0.04em) at sizes between 11px and 13px for immediate scannability on badges and certification tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt rhythmic base inside a responsive 12-column grid container with a maximum desktop width of 1320px.

- **Desktop (≥ 1024px)**: 12-column layout with 24px/1.5rem gutters and 32px canvas margins. Category rows display 6 to 8 items horizontally; product carousels showcase 5 to 6 cards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 16px gutters and 24px margins. Spec sheets and wholesale lead capture forms adapt to stacked halves.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 12px gutters and 16px safe margins. Category roundels and best-seller rails transition into smooth-scrolling horizontal cards with subtle edge-bleed peek cues.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Internal card padding uses `space-md` (16px), banner heroes use `space-xl` (40px) vertical padding, and interactive form groups maintain `space-sm` (8px) gaps between labels and inputs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses plush, pillowy elevation rather than stark drop shadows, reinforcing a safe, friendly toy universe:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Inset)**: Tinted pastel wells (`#F8FAFC`, `#EFF6FF`, `#FFF7ED`) with subtle hairline outlines (`#E2E8F0` or 15% opacity primary/secondary colors).
- **Level 1 (Card Rest)**: Product grids and category icons rest with `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` wrapped in a 1px soft border (`#F1F5F9`).
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Product Card)**: Cards lift on cursor interaction: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Lead Modals)**: Multi-step B2B quote sheets and sample requisition dialogues sit atop a 40% dimmed backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) using an expansive soft drop shadow: `0 25px 50px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces softened geometric radii, ensuring all corners look ergonomic and child-friendly:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Standard input fields, quantity counter buttons, tabular spec cells, and small status tags.
- **Card & Banner Radius (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` and `rounded-xl` / `1.5rem`)**: Product cards, category tiles, and feature callout blocks.
- **Large Container Radius (`rounded-2xl` / `1.5rem` - `2rem`)**: B2B sample inquiry dialogs, hero banners, and value-proposition bands.
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full`)**: Primary action buttons ("Add to Cart", "Submit Request"), carousel navigation circles, pill notification count badges, and verification status indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Retail Primary**: Full pill shape, filled with primary coral (`#E11D48`), white bold text, accompanied by directional arrow or cart iconography. Hover activates `#BE123C` with subtle scale transition (1.02).
- **B2B Secondary**: Electric blue (`#2563EB`) solid button for business conversions ("Get a Sample (B2B)", "Submit Request"). Hover state darkens to `#1D4ED8`.
- **Wholesale Outlined**: White surface with a 1.5px solid `#2563EB` border, royal blue typography, and pill curvature.
- **Quantity Selector**: Segmented horizontal pill with gentle `#F1F5F9` background, minus/plus icon buttons, and centered bold numeral.

### Badges & Pill Chips
- **Discount Badges**: Solid coral red (`#FFE4E6` background with `#E11D48` bold text) placed on top corners of product photos.
- **Safety / BIS Verification Badges**: Soft mint fill (`#ECFDF5`) with emerald text (`#047857`) and green check shield icon.
- **B2B Status Tag**: Dual-tone amber/sky pill (`#FEF3C7` with `#92400E` label) designating items available for bulk wholesale tiers.

### Product Cards
- **Structure**: Clean white container with `rounded-xl` borders, 1px `#F1F5F9` edge, and subtle top-right wishlist heart toggle.
- **Image Staging**: Clean, neutral, soft-shadowed toy renders centered in a 1:1 aspect ratio with pastel tinted background on hover.
- **Price Block**: Prominent bold sales price with strike-through MRP and red percentage-off callout. Bottom pinned with full-width primary or outline CTA.

### Input Fields & Modals
- **Form Controls**: Crisp white background, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, 8px corner radius, and slate placeholder. Focus state blooms with a 3px `#BFDBFE` glow ring and `#2563EB` border.
- **Two-Column Inquiry Modals**: Left illustrative side panel featuring pastel sky background (`#EFF6FF`), brand trust anchors, and manufacturer certifications; right column structured with numerical stepped progress bars and paired field grids.

### Specification & Trust Grids
- **Spec Tables**: Alternating soft rows (`#F8FAFC` vs `#FFFFFF`) with muted metric names and bold specification outputs.
- **Trust Badges Bar**: Horizontal card strip highlighting "Safe & Non-Toxic", "Durable Quality", "Fast Dispatch", and "Dedicated B2B Support" using tinted dual-ring icon badges.
